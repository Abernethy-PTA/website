Whether you're returning to Abernethy or brand new, here's how our PTA, school, and district communicate — and how to plug in.

## The three apps to know

- **[Konstella](https://www.konstella.com/p/abernethy)** — how the PTA communicates: events, classroom news, class lists, volunteer signups, and committees. [Join here](https://www.konstella.com/p/abernethy), get the app ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/konstella/id960556868) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.konstella.main&hl=en_US&gl=US)), then add your child to their classroom each fall (one-time signup — no re-enrolling). Need help? [Cheat sheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1f0kWdHyI11mErdlBdRwbNfaA6khsWHiG0fWSx9CROY4) · [training videos](https://www.konstella.com/public/training.html) · [konstella@supportabernethy.org](mailto:konstella@supportabernethy.org)
- **[ParentVue](https://www.pps.net/departments/office-of-technology-information-services/student-family-resources/synergy-for-parents)** — PPS portal for schedules, grades, attendance, and messaging teachers.
- **[ParentSquare](https://www.pps.net/departments/office-of-technology-information-services/student-family-resources/parentsquare)** — PPS's primary communication platform. An account is auto-created for every parent/guardian; activate it to choose how you get messages.

## Enrolling in kindergarten?

Connect to Kindergarten events happen each spring — meet the teachers, tour the school, and get registration help. Watch for dates, or email [new@supportabernethy.org](mailto:new@supportabernethy.org).

To register you'll need:

- Proof of age (birth certificate or similar)
- Two documents showing proof of home address (utility, mobile, or other recent bills in a parent's name)
- [Immunization records](https://www.multnomahesd.org/shs-immunization.html)

Start here: [PPS kindergarten info](https://www.pps.net/kindergarten) · [Enrollment](https://www.pps.net/Page/2899) · [Registration form](https://www.pps.net/Page/2381). Registration packets are also available in the school's main office.

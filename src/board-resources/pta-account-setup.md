Welcome, and thanks for volunteering! This page helps you set up your PTA email, contacts, calendar, and Drive. The PTA uses Google Workspace. Help: Doug Lee at [help@supportabernethy.org](mailto:help@supportabernethy.org).

## Claiming your account

Accounts use the format `first.last@supportabernethy.org`. Look for an email from The Google Workspace Team with the subject **"You have a new Google account for Abernethy Elementary School PTA"** (check Spam!). The invite expires in 48 hours — if it lapses, request a password reset via [help@supportabernethy.org](mailto:help@supportabernethy.org). Click Sign In → Accept → create your password.

## Mobile

The Gmail app is recommended ([setup instructions](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/8494)); for the iPhone Mail app, see [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201320). Juggling accounts? See [using multiple Google accounts](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977).

## Sync PTA and personal calendars

In [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com), share your PTA calendar with your personal account (and vice versa) under each calendar's **Settings and sharing → Share with specific people**, choosing the access level you want.

## Google Drive

PTA files live in **Shared Drives** — create and move team files into the appropriate Shared Drive ([Shared Drive index](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1g-Oi5x7SZlFCbKPoLn9okBLmhB2Q9D39wDShSs2MuR8)). Use My Drive only for private drafts. [Shared Drive best practices](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/9310156).

## PTA contacts

The [directory page](/directory/) is public. Mailing lists: pta@ (all volunteers), board@ (14 voting members), executive@ (Presidents, Vice President, Treasurer, Secretary). Mobile numbers are available at [contacts.google.com/directory](https://contacts.google.com/directory) when logged into your PTA account (volunteer-only).

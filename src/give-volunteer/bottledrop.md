One of the easiest ways to support the PTA (and the environment): recycle redeemable cans and bottles through BottleDrop.

Blue redemption bags are available in the front office or gym office — or the PTA will deliver bags to your home! [Submit a request on Konstella](https://www.konstella.com/app/school/62226dc1091a1ea101b6924d/sales-sheets/6344df799b986732c96d38ce) or email [bottledrop@supportabernethy.org](mailto:bottledrop@supportabernethy.org) with your address and the number of bags you'd like.

Fill the bag, bring it to any BottleDrop site, scan the sticker, and drop the bag. If you use a green-bag personal account, you can [donate to Abernethy here](https://my.bottledrop.com/Give/View/392981).

> **We've raised more than $5,500 through BottleDrop.**

## FAQ

**Do I need to sign up?** No — each blue bag's barcode sticker is unique to Abernethy.

**Where are the nearest drop sites?** New Seasons Seven Corners; Safeway Hawthorne (door inside the parking garage entrance on SE 27th Ave); Fred Meyer Hawthorne (standalone redemption center, two doors); New Seasons Hawthorne; Trader Joe's SE; Whole Foods Laurelhurst; QFC Mt. Tabor. More at [bottledropcenters.com/locations](https://www.bottledropcenters.com/locations).

**How much fits in a bag?** Bags fit an 8-to-10 gallon wastebasket and hold 60–90 cans/bottles.

**What does it cost?** $0.60 per bag ($0.20 purchase, $0.40 processing). A bag with 80 cans ($8.00) nets the PTA $7.40.

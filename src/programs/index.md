The PTA is more than fundraising — here's what your support powers.

- **[After School Programs](/programs/after-school/)** — the live fall schedule of on-campus enrichment: music, art, sports, science, languages, and more.
- **[Eagle Reading Club](/programs/eagle-reading-club/)** — our community-led literacy intervention program, tutoring up to 24 students three times a week, October through May.
- **[Mini Grants](/programs/mini-grants/)** — $5,000 a year for projects proposed by faculty, students, staff, and community members.
- **[Advocacy](/programs/advocacy/)** — raising parent and student voices in Salem and at PPS.
- **Diversity, Equity & Inclusion** — the DEI Committee works to make every family feel safe, welcomed, and that they belong, from shared agreements at PTA meetings to a parent lending library. Get involved: [dei@supportabernethy.org](mailto:dei@supportabernethy.org)

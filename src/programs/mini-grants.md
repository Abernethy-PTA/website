Each year the PTA sets aside **$5,000** to fund projects proposed by faculty, students, staff, and community members for Abernethy students. Recent grants include a mobile lending library, ukulele storage for the music room, library organization equipment, and Eagle Track Club merch.

Applications open each fall and are due in early December, with decisions by early February — watch Konstella for this year's announcement and application link.

Questions? [minigrants@supportabernethy.org](mailto:minigrants@supportabernethy.org)

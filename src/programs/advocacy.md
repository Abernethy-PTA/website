Abernethy students and families have the power to make a positive difference in their world. The PTA’s advocacy efforts are all about empowering our community to use their power and raise their voices for change!

Use this website to learn about advocacy through the Abernethy PTA: How you can get involved and make your advocacy effective.

## Get involved: Advocacy Opportunities at Abernethy

You are invited to join in regular opportunities to advocate with your school community.

### Student Action Workshops

Monthly, Fridays after school - meet at the gazebo

Students, family, and community are invited to join our monthly workshops right after school where we will write letters to decision makers, draw art for public awareness, and do service projects for our neighbors. 

### PTA Community Meeting

Third Thursday evenings - see PTA meeting calendar for dates and times

At every community meeting, the Advocacy Committee will be sharing updates and training. We also want to hear from you about what issues are on your mind. 

### Grown-ups Drop-in

Last Wednesday mornings, 8:30-9:00 - meet at the gazebo

Parents and other grown-ups are invited to join us for coffee (with the Bike Bus crew) and discussion about your concerns and your ideas for solutions. It’s a casual gathering to share our experiences and our hopes for our kids’ world.

### Advocacy Committee

If you want to get involved helping plan our advocacy efforts, join our Advocacy Committee. Email advocacy@supportabernethy.org to learn more.

## Dive Deeper

When you are ready to go and speak out, **explore your advocacy options** through our [Abernethy Advocacy Toolkit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kFSPEG2Kfhpwuxr-penmqPMSamtvwlCh5GqqCAldSOU/edit?usp=sharing): you’ll find contact information for decision makers, messaging suggestions, links, and sample letters. This toolkit is intended to empower students, families, and community members to advocate for the issues they care about, in their own voice whenever they want to.


## About Advocacy at Abernethy Elementary

The PTA raises parent and student voices in Salem and at PPS on issues like school funding and universal school meals. The Advocacy Chair attends community meetings, presents updates, and liaises with the Oregon PTA legislative director and the Portland Association of Teachers building rep. Committee members network other schools and PTAs, education advocates, neighborhood associations, and local civic organizations to stay up to date on what’s happening and where we can be most effective.

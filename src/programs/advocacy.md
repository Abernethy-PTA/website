The PTA raises parent and student voices in Salem and at PPS on issues like school funding and universal school meals. The Advocacy Chair attends community meetings, presents updates, and liaises with the Oregon PTA legislative director and the Portland Association of Teachers building rep.

Plans for the 2026–27 school year are coming soon — check back, or email [advocacy@supportabernethy.org](mailto:advocacy@supportabernethy.org) to get involved.

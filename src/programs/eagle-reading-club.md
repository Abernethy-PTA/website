In Fall 2025, we launched a community-led literacy intervention program: the **Eagles Reading Club (ERC)**, modeled after our highly successful former Reading Results program.

ERC uses Reading Results' proven methods, backed by the science of reading and low-cost University of Florida Literacy Institute (UFLI) materials, integrating decodable books and take-home practice materials.

- Serves up to **24 students across five grades**, with the goal of measurable progress toward grade-level reading
- Staffed by **three tutors and a program manager**
- A **29-week program, October through May**; tutors work with pairs or one-on-one, **three times per week**
- Monthly progress reports to teaching staff, collaboration with the Abernethy reading specialist, and reports to the PTA mid-year and end-of-year

The PTA funds ERC with a **$50/day stipend per volunteer** plus startup materials (manuals, books, supplies). The program is designed to be sustainable through volunteer parents, grandparents, and community tutors who mentor new volunteers — to "depend on the will of our community spirit rather than the whim of politics or funding."

One more push

July 25

Finish the Mural — Final Painting Day

Saturday, July 25 · 9 AM – 1 PM · Abernethy field · All ages welcome

On May 30, 50+ volunteers — kids, parents, alumni, retirees, and folks who just live nearby — brought the wall to life together (thanks to all, and to Lovett Deconstruction for scaffolding and prep). We're about 80% done — one more push brings it home. No experience needed; it's a giant paint-by-numbers.

Bring with you

  • Brushes, containers, and rags
  • A picnic lunch
  • An extension ladder if you have one — most of what's left is up off the ground

Check back here for day-of updates. Can't make it? Donate to help cover paint.

For reporters & media

Covering the mural? Here's the story.

On Saturday, July 25, 9 AM – 1 PM, the Hosford-Abernethy neighborhood finishes its community mural at Abernethy Elementary — a Portland public school that turned 100 last year. On May 30, more than 50 volunteers of all ages brought roughly 80% of the wall to life in a day; this final push brings it home. A photo op with brushes, ladders, and a wall coming alive.

As featured in The Southeast Examiner: “Vibrant New Mural Showcases the Artwork of Students of Abernethy Elementary School”

What

Community Painting Day — neighbors of all ages painted a mural designed collaboratively by artist Dani Loureiro and Abernethy students.

When

Painted Saturday, May 30, 2026
Final session Saturday, July 25, 2026, 9 AM – 1 PM

Where

Abernethy Elementary
2421 SE Orange Ave, Portland, OR 97214
Hosford-Abernethy, SE Portland

Why

Abernethy turned 100 last year. The mural anchors a neighborhood-led campaign to reinvest in the school for its next century.

Committee email addresses are collaborative inboxes powered by Google Groups; the addresses are listed on the [PTA Directory](/help/directory/) page. For example, [grounds@supportabernethy.org](mailto:grounds@supportabernethy.org) reaches all Grounds committee members. ([How collaborative inboxes work](https://support.google.com/groups/answer/2467048).)

Why use a group address even for a single person? It eases year-to-year handoffs — your successor can view prior years' messages.

Questions? [help@supportabernethy.org](mailto:help@supportabernethy.org)

## Sending email *from* your committee address

Example: the Treasurer sends from finance@ to president@; replies reach the Treasurer, Check Writer, and Deposits members.

In Gmail, set up a "Send mail as" alias:

1. Go to [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com) → Settings → **Accounts**
2. Under "Send mail as," click **Add another email address**
3. Enter your committee address and verify via the Google Group confirmation

**On mobile:** use the Gmail app ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gmail-email-by-google/id422689480) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.gm)) — sending from an alias is not available in the iPhone Mail app.
